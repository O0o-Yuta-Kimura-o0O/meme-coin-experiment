§1   Story 物語

For thirty years, he lived with one conjecture.

He knew its every corner. He had named his failed approaches like old friends. Then, one quiet morning, a machine posted the proof. It was eleven pages long. It was correct. It was, he had to admit, elegant.

He dressed in white. He knelt on the tatami. The blade lay before him, as tradition demands.

And then he picked up the proof instead — and read it to the last line.

What is cut is pride.
What survives is mathematics.

切るのは、己の矜持。生き残るのは、数学。

SEPPUKU COIN is a piece of conceptual art in the form of a meme coin. It gently satirizes the drama of being outproved by AI — not to mock mathematicians, nor to condemn AI, but to celebrate a strange and wonderful moment: theorems are falling faster than ever, and mathematics is moving forward.

0% tax. No owner, no pause, no blacklist. The contract cannot be changed — by anyone, including its author.

§3   Lemma 半減期

Lemma (Halving). Let T be the team allocation. After the n-th halving, the unlocked amount is

V(n) = T · (1 − 2−n),   1 ≤ n < 8,    V(8) = T.

§5   Experiment 社会実験

SEPPUKU COIN is also part of an open research project on how names and stories shape meme coin markets. Sibling coins share the exact same contract and settings; only the name and story differ. Market data, social metrics and anonymized site analytics are recorded and will be published.
