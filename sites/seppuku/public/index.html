§1   Story 物語

For thirty years, he lived with one conjecture.

He knew its every corner. He had named his failed approaches like old friends. Then, one quiet morning, a machine posted the proof. It was eleven pages long. It was correct. It was, he had to admit, elegant.

He dressed in white. He knelt on the tatami. The blade lay before him, as tradition demands.

And then he picked up the proof instead — and read it to the last line.

What is cut is pride.
What survives is mathematics.

切るのは、己の矜持。生き残るのは、数学。

$SEPPUKU is a piece of conceptual art in the form of a meme coin. It gently satirizes the drama of being outproved by AI — not to mock mathematicians, nor to condemn AI, but to celebrate a strange and wonderful moment: theorems are falling faster than ever, and mathematics is moving forward.

$SEPPUKU は、ミームコインという形をとったコンセプチュアルアートです。AI に先を越された数学者の悲劇を客観的に、少しだけ皮肉を込めて描きます。ただしこれは、数学者への嘲笑でも AI への否定でもありません。AI によって数学が前に進むことを喜ぶ、一人のアマチュア数学者からの前向きなメッセージです。

§3   Lemma 半減期

Lemma (Halving). Let T be the team allocation. After the n-th halving, the unlocked amount is

V(n) = T · (1 − 2−n),   1 ≤ n < 8,    V(8) = T.

Proof. Written in the contract, verifiable by anyone. The first halving comes 90 days after launch; each subsequent one, 180 days later. ∎

§5   Experiment 社会実験

$SEPPUKU is also part of an open research project on how names and stories shape meme coin markets. Sibling coins share the exact same contract and settings; only the name and story differ. Market data, social metrics and anonymized site analytics are recorded and will be published.

$SEPPUKU は、「名前とストーリーがミームコインの市場にどう影響するか」を調べる公開研究の一部でもあります。同じコントラクト・同じ設定で、名前とストーリーだけが違うコインと比較します。
