The story 物語

The samurai did not panic-sell. When honor was on the line, they held — to the very end.

$SEPPUKU is a meme coin for holders with that kind of resolve. And it asks the same of its creator: the team's tokens are locked in a contract and released only by halving, like Bitcoin. No quick exit. No secret mint. The resolve is written in code, for anyone to verify.

侍は狼狽売りをしない。$SEPPUKU は、覚悟あるホルダーのためのミームコイン。発行者の取り分も半減期でしか解放されず、その覚悟はコードとして誰でも確認できる。

Team halving schedule 半減期

After a 90-day cliff, half of the team allocation unlocks. Every 180 days after that, half of what remains unlocks. Fully unlocked after the 8th halving (~3.7 years).

How to buy 購入方法

  1. Get a wallet. Coinbase Wallet, MetaMask, Rabby — anything that supports Base.
  2. Get ETH on Base. Buy on an exchange and withdraw to the Base network, or bridge from Ethereum.

This is an experiment 社会実験

$SEPPUKU is part of an open research project on how names and stories shape meme coin markets. Sibling coins share the exact same contract and settings; only the name and story differ. Market data, social metrics and anonymized site analytics are recorded and will be published.

$SEPPUKU は「名前とストーリーがミームコインの市場にどう影響するか」を調べる公開研究の一部です。同じコントラクト・同じ設定で、名前とストーリーだけが違うコインと比較します。
